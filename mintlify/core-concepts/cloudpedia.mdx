---
title: Cloudpedia
description: Use Cloudpedia to see company updates, projects, and decisions in one place.
---

Cloudpedia is Cloudberry's simple company overview. It turns processed activity from your connected tools into three useful views.

## Updates

A timeline of activity received from Slack, Linear, GitHub, and other configured sources.

## Projects

Projects and repositories found in company activity, with a simple status such as planning, in progress, or blocked when the available context supports it.

## Decisions

Important choices and changes to earlier choices identified in company activity.

## How it stays current

Cloudpedia updates after a connected event has been processed. If processing is temporarily unavailable, an update may appear after the worker catches up.

Cloudpedia is a view of your company context, not the original source. Slack remains the source for Slack messages, Linear for issues, and GitHub for code activity.

## Best uses

- Get a quick view of what changed recently.
- Find projects that may need attention.
- Review decisions without searching every channel.
- Open Chat when you need a detailed answer.
