---
title: Workspaces
description: Understand how Cloudberry keeps each company's data separate.
---

A Cloudberry workspace belongs to an organization. Members of that organization share its connected tools, knowledge, chats, and Cloudpedia view.

## Members

Cloudberry uses your signed-in account and its organization membership to decide which workspace you can access. You do not select an organization by typing an ID into the browser.

## Owners

Organization owners manage connections to Slack, Linear, GitHub, and Codex. Owners can also retry a connection when a provider needs attention or disconnect a provider.

## Privacy

Each company's integrations, events, chats, knowledge, and computer status are organization-scoped. The browser communicates with Cloudberry's API; it does not connect directly to the private knowledge database.

## Current limits

The current workspace selects the first organization membership available for a user. A user-facing switcher for people who belong to multiple organizations is not available yet.
