Introduction

Cloudberry is the company brain for connected work.

It brings the context from the tools your team already uses into one workspace, so people can ask questions, find decisions, and understand what is happening without searching every tool separately.

What you can do

Ask questions

Use Cloudberry Chat to ask about projects, decisions, tasks, conversations, and code changes. Cloudberry searches your company's connected context before creating an answer.

Connect tools

Connect Slack, Linear, and GitHub to keep company knowledge up to date. Cloudberry also supports Codex for users who want to use their connected OpenAI account for chat.

The bigger picture

Cloudpedia gives your team a simple view of recent updates, projects, and decisions collected from connected tools.

Shared context

Every workspace is separated by organization. Your team's data is not mixed with another company's data.

Next steps

  1. Set up your workspace
  2. Connect Slack, Linear, or GitHub
  3. Ask your first question
  4. Learn about Cloudpedia

Important note

Cloudberry currently learns from new events delivered by connected tools. It does not yet import every historical message, reply directly inside Slack or Linear, or operate a browser/computer on your behalf.
