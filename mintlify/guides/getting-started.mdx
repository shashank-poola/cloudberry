---
title: Getting started
description: Set up Cloudberry and get your team ready to use it.
---

Follow these steps to get a Cloudberry workspace ready for your team.

## Sign in

Open your Cloudberry workspace and sign in with Google or GitHub through Supabase Auth.

Email and password sign-up is not enabled in the current setup.

## Open Plugins

After signing in, open **Plugins** in the Cloudberry dashboard. This is where an organization owner connects Slack, Linear, or GitHub.

## Connect tools

Choose a provider and complete its connection flow. Cloudberry uses Composio to handle provider authorization.

Start with the tools where your team keeps the most important context. For example:

- Slack for conversations and team decisions.
- Linear for issues and project work.
- GitHub for commits and repository activity.

See [Integrations](/integrations/overview) for provider-specific setup.

## Wait for updates

Cloudberry receives new events from the triggers configured for your workspace. Once they are processed, they become available in Chat and Cloudpedia.

The current product does not automatically import the complete history of a provider.

## Ask a question

Open Chat and ask a question about your company. Try:

```text
What changed on the launch project this week?
```

```text
What decisions have we made about the billing migration?
```

See [Using Chat](/guides/using-chat) for tips.

## Self-hosted teams

If your team runs Cloudberry from the repository, the system also needs a Supabase project, a FalkorDB database, the private knowledge service, and the background worker. Keep those services private and follow the deployment notes in the repository README.
